MAISON VELOIR

We have been here since 1947.
So has the silence.

Born in the arrondissements of post-war Paris, VELOIR was founded on a single conviction: that the finest things resist announcement. Our ateliers have remained in the 8th ever since.

In an era dominated by urgency and visual clamor, our artisans continue to practice an architecture of restraint. Every seam is considered; every grain of calfskin is selected by hand under northern natural light. True luxury requires neither emblem nor volume.
